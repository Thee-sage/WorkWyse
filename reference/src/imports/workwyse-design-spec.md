Design a complete visual style and landing page interface for a startup platform called **WorkWyse**.

WorkWyse is a job transparency platform where job seekers report their application experiences to help identify ghost job listings, fake postings, repeated reposted jobs, and misleading hiring processes.

The design should feel **artistic, editorial, and thoughtful**, not like a typical SaaS dashboard.

Overall design concept:
The platform should feel like a **public archive of hiring experiences** or an **investigative workspace** where documents are reviewed and verified by a community.

Visual language:
Reports feel like documents or case files.
Trust scores feel like verification stamps or seals.
Companies appear as archived records.
User confirmations appear like annotations or signatures on documents.

Style requirements:

* modern editorial style interface
* soft paper-like background
* subtle grain or texture
* vector illustrations integrated into the layout
* slightly hand-drawn or sketch-like accents
* soft shadows for depth
* minimalist design with large whitespace
* artistic but clean and professional

Illustration style:
Create simple vector illustrations that feel editorial and slightly hand-drawn.

Possible elements include:

* job application document
* verification stamp
* warning marks for ghost jobs
* highlight strokes and annotations
* sticky notes
* file folders representing company reports
* community confirmation icons

Typography:
Use an editorial style headline font combined with a modern clean sans-serif body font.

Color palette:

* cream or off-white paper background
* dark charcoal text
* muted red or orange accent for warnings
* soft gray shadows
* minimal colors overall

Layout structure:

Hero section:
Large artistic illustration showing a job application document being reviewed with annotations and stamps.

Headline:
"Stop Applying Into The Void"

Subheading explaining that WorkWyse helps job seekers identify misleading hiring processes.

Call to action button:
"Submit a Report"

Below the hero:
Feature sections with illustrations explaining:

* report your experience
* community verification
* transparent company records

Create reusable components including:

* report cards
* company profile layout
* trust score badge
* report submission interface

The final design should feel calm, trustworthy, and distinctive, like a thoughtful editorial product rather than a corporate dashboard.
